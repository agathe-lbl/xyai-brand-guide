02 The Brand

Three locked
frame shifts.

These are non-negotiable. They define how XY.AI Labs is positioned in every context — from LinkedIn posts to investor decks. Never deviate from these frames.

01
AI-native company.
XY.AI is an AI company that operates in healthcare — not a healthcare company that uses AI.
Not a healthcare company · Not an AI-powered tool
02
Compiled AI.
Agents are trained and compiled — not LLMs prompted at runtime. This is the central technical differentiator.
Not LLM-at-runtime · Not prompt engineering
03
Tasks, not jobs.
Reframe automation as eliminating task-level friction — not replacing human roles. People stay. Busywork doesn't.
Not "replacing jobs" · Not "eliminating roles"

Do

  • Use on high-contrast backgrounds
  • Maintain minimum clear space (height of "Y" on all sides)

Don't

08 Imagery

The bot is
the hero.

The iridescent orb is XY.AI's primary visual asset. It represents the compiled AI agent — living, learning, in motion. Photography supports; the bot leads.

The Bot

Iridescent sphere — blue, purple, orange. Used in hero positions, motion assets, and product UI. Never stretch, recolor, or crop the orb. It has two white "eye" slots — never obscure them.

Photography

Real operational environments. Clinicians at work — not posed. No stethoscope + laptop stock photos. Data and interfaces are supporting characters, not hero images.

Compliance Badges

SOC 2 (AICPA) + HIPAA Compliant — both certified. Use on product pages, sales decks, and footers. Never modify or recolor the badges.

09 Motion

The bot
breathes.

Motion is used to bring the bot mascot to life. The iridescent orb rotates and pulses — suggesting intelligence in motion, not mechanical automation.

10 Do

What works.

Patterns that are on-brand across voice, structure, and visual. Repeat these.

Voice
✓Open with personal experience or observation
✓Use deliberate sentence fragments for emphasis
✓Let one idea breathe per post
✓Acknowledge the giants before you differentiate
✓End on conviction or human stakes — not metrics
✓Mix emotional language with operational data
Structure & Visual
✓1–3 sentences per paragraph on LinkedIn
✓Key declarative statements get their own line
✓Anecdotes lead; insight follows
✓Use black + white as primary palette
✓Bot as visual hero — with breathing room
✓Clean typographic layouts over complex infographics
11 Don't

What breaks
the brand.

Anti-patterns. Any of these in a draft is a flag — stop and rewrite before it goes to Sam.

Voice
✕"Moonshot," "game-changing," "disruptive," "10x"
✕Dry or academic register — no abstraction without stakes
✕"Not because X, but because Y" sentence structure
✕Explicit CTA — "What do you think?" / "Drop a comment"
✕Mixing two subjects in one post
✕Token maxing in thought leadership posts
Visual & Topic
✕Partner names (CollabMD) in thought leadership
✕Headers or bold blocks in LinkedIn posts
✕Generic AI imagery (glowing brains, neural nets)
✕Stock healthcare photography (stethoscope + laptop)
✕Recoloring or stretching the bot or logo
✕Repeating topics Sam has already published on
